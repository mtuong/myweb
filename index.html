Managing IT infrastructure and digital systems for the Oxford University Clinical Research Unit's infectious disease research across Southeast Asia

Get in Touch

I am an IT Manager at the Oxford University Clinical Research Unit (OUCRU), a leading clinical and public health research organization. OUCRU operates with a mission of Local Research with Global Impact, conducting groundbreaking research on infectious diseases across Southeast Asia with offices in Vietnam, Indonesia, and Nepal.

In my role, I support OUCRU's mission by managing IT infrastructure, digital systems, and technology solutions that enable our researchers to conduct critical studies on diseases like tuberculosis, antimicrobial resistance, and other infectious diseases. I'm committed to leveraging technology to support research excellence and advance global health.

OUCRU's achievements include 100+ completed clinical trials, 2000+ publications, and 100+ PhD graduates who contribute to health research worldwide. I take pride in being part of this globally impactful organization.
